## 🤖 GEO Optimization (AI Citability)

### 5-Pillar Citability Checklist (Every Article)
| Pillar | Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **Answer-First** | H2 = question, 1st paragraph = 40-60 word direct answer | Template enforced in brief |
| **Stats & Quotes** | ≥2 statistics with sources (ГОСТ, internal data, expert) | SME interview + data |
| **Tables & Lists** | ≥1 comparison table OR ≥2 structured lists | Mandatory in brief |
| **Question Headings** | All H2/H3 as user questions (PAA format) | SEO brief + PAA research |
| **E-E-A-T Signals** | Author (technologist), dateModified, cases, production photos | Author box + schema |

### Quality Gates (Pre-Publish)
```
☐ SEO Brief approved (keywords, intent, competitors)
☐ SME interviewed (expert quotes, facts)
☐ Draft written (word count, structure)
☐ GEO Checklist passed (5 pillars ≥ 80/100)
☐ Anti-AI-slop: 0 em-dashes, no fluff, specific facts
☐ Schema valid (Rich Results Test)
☐ Internal links: 3-5 to money pages
☐ Images: alt, WebP, <200KB, lazyload, width/height
☐ CTA present (calculator / form / phone)
☐ Author box + dateModified
```
